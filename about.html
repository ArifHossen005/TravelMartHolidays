Our Story

A Decade of Dreams Delivered

Founded in 2015 in Dhaka, TravelMate Holiday started with one simple mission — to make spiritual and leisure travel accessible, organized, and truly memorable for Bangladeshis.

What began as a small Umrah operator has grown into a full-service travel agency handling Umrah packages, international tours across Asia and the Middle East, and curated domestic experiences.

Over the years, we have served hundreds of families, groups, and solo travelers — earning their trust through transparent pricing, dedicated support, and seamless end-to-end service.
